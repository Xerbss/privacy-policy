Privacy Policy

Cats vs Dogs — developed by Piegeon

Last updated: June 6, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Piegeon ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Cats vs Dogs (the "App"), available on the Google Play Store. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Cats vs Dogs does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from its users. The App does not require account registration, does not request your name, email address, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or tracking services. No data is sent to our servers or to any third party.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App may save game progress (such as level completion and settings) locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. Children's Privacy

Cats vs Dogs is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). Since the App collects no personal data whatsoever, it is safe for use by children.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect user data. There are no advertisements, in-app purchases, or external network requests made by the App.

5. Permissions

The App requests only the minimum permissions required for gameplay. Any permissions used are solely for core game functionality and are not used to collect or transmit personal information.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any user data, there is no personal data at risk of a security breach from our side. Local game data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

7. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data because we do not collect any. Local game data stored on your device can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data via your device settings.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, delete, or port. If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California and have any concerns, you are welcome to contact us and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
