Privacy Policy

Cats vs Dogs — developed by Piegeon

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Piegeon ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Cats vs Dogs (the "App"), available on the Google Play Store. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Cats vs Dogs does not require account registration and does not directly request your name, email address, or precise location. However, the App uses third-party advertising services (Google AdMob and Unity Ads / LevelPlay mediation) to display ads, and these services automatically collect certain data as described below.

Data type Collected Shared with third parties Purpose
Device or other IDs (e.g. advertising ID) Yes Yes Advertising or marketing
App interactions Yes Yes Advertising or marketing
Other actions Yes Yes Advertising or marketing
Other app performance data (diagnostics) Yes Yes Analytics
Crash logs Yes No App functionality (identifying and fixing bugs)
Approximate location (derived from IP) Yes Yes Advertising or marketing

None of this data is processed ephemerally — it may be retained by us or our service providers for as long as needed to serve its purpose. This data collection is required for the App's advertising and stability functions and cannot be individually disabled within the App, though you can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier through your device's system settings.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App may save game progress (such as level completion and settings) locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. This local save data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in Section 1:

Google AdMob — serves ads within the App. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Unity Ads / LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation — serves and mediates ads within the App. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

These services may use your advertising identifier and app interaction data to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. Where required by applicable law (such as in the EEA and UK), the App presents a consent form before any personalized data collection or ad personalization occurs.

4. Children's Privacy

If Cats vs Dogs is directed at or accessible to children, we are committed to complying with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is described in this policy, and ad serving in child-directed contexts is handled in a manner consistent with those requirements.

5. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions required for gameplay and for its advertising and diagnostic functions described above. Permissions are not used to collect personal information beyond what is disclosed in this policy.

6. Data Security

All data collected by the App and its third-party SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (TLS). Local game data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

7. Data Retention

Because the App does not require an account, we do not maintain a personal user data profile on our own servers. Data collected by our third-party advertising and analytics providers is retained according to their own data retention practices, as described in their respective privacy policies linked above. Local game data stored on your device can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data via your device settings.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of data collected about you by our advertising partners. Since the App does not maintain accounts or personally identifiable profiles on our own servers, most such requests are best directed to Google (AdMob) or Unity via the privacy policies linked above, or you may reset/limit your device's advertising identifier through your device's system settings. If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California and have any concerns, you are welcome to contact us and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
