Cats vs Dogs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Piegeon ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Cats vs Dogs mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Advertising & Diagnostic Data

The App displays ads using a mediation setup that includes Google AdMob and Unity Ads (LevelPlay mediation), along with any additional networks in their mediation chains. AppLovin MAX is integrated as an additional mediation partner but is not yet active; this policy will be updated once it begins serving ads. To serve and measure ads, and to keep the App stable, these SDKs automatically collect the data described below:

Data type Collected Shared with third parties Purpose
Device or other IDs (e.g. advertising ID) Yes Yes Advertising or marketing
App interactions Yes Yes Advertising or marketing
Other actions Yes Yes Advertising or marketing
Other app performance data (diagnostics) Yes Yes Analytics
Crash logs Yes No App functionality (identifying and fixing bugs)
Approximate location (derived from IP) Yes Yes Advertising or marketing

This data is collected and processed by our ad mediation partners according to their own privacy policies, not by us directly. You can review their practices at policies.google.com/privacy (Google AdMob) and unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy (Unity Ads). Once AppLovin MAX becomes active, its privacy policy will also be linked here.

None of this data is processed ephemerally — it may be retained by these providers for as long as needed to serve its purpose. Collection is required for the App's advertising and stability functions and cannot be individually disabled within the App, though you can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier through your device's system settings.

Game Progress & Cloud Save

On Android, the App uses Google Play Games Services to let you sign in with your Google account and sync your game progress (level completion, unlocks, settings) across devices. If you don't sign in, or while offline, progress is saved locally on your device using standard storage mechanisms. See Section 2 below for what this sign-in accesses.

Information We Don't Collect

We do not require account creation to play the App (Google sign-in for cloud save is optional — see Section 2). The App does not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information beyond what Google Play Games Services provides for sign-in, and we do not currently use in-app purchases in this App.

2. Google User Data

On Android, the App uses Google Play Games Services Sign-In so you can save your progress to the cloud and sync it across devices. This is optional — you can play the App fully without signing in, in which case no Google Account data is accessed and progress is saved locally only. If you do sign in, the section below describes exactly what happens, in the specific terms Google requests for API/OAuth review.

Data Accessed: Your Google Play Games player ID, public display name, and profile photo, provided automatically by Google Play Games Services when you sign in. We do not request your email address, contacts, Drive files, or any other Google data. The App does not use leaderboards or achievements, so no gameplay activity is shared to a public profile.

Data Usage: This data is used solely to identify your save file and sync your game progress (level completion, unlocks, settings) across your devices via Google's Saved Games API.

Data Sharing: We do not share this data with any third party, including our advertising partners. It is only exchanged with Google as necessary to operate Play Games Services sign-in and cloud save.

Data Storage & Protection: Your save data and Play Games profile information are stored on Google's infrastructure (Play Games Services / Saved Games API), not on our own servers. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Data Retention & Deletion: This data is retained for as long as your Google account remains linked to the App. You can unlink it at any time from within the App's settings, or delete your Play Games activity entirely via your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com). You can also contact us at the email in Section 10 to request deletion.

If a future update adds new Google API scopes (for example, leaderboards or achievements), we will update this section and, where required, prompt you for consent before the change takes effect.

3. How We Use Information & Legal Basis

Any data described above is used solely to display and measure advertising, and to diagnose and fix technical issues. We do not sell your personal information.

Where GDPR applies (EEA/UK users), we and our ad partners rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for personalized/targeted advertising and any non-essential tracking identifiers. Where required, a consent prompt is shown before this occurs, and you can withdraw consent at any time (see Section 7).
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for non-personalized (contextual) advertising needed to keep the App free, and for crash/diagnostic logs needed to keep the App functioning correctly.

The App does not use any data described above to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. Ad selection may involve automated, algorithm-based targeting by our ad partners, but this does not amount to profiling with legal effect.

4. Children's Privacy

Cats vs Dogs is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

5. Data Retention

We do not maintain a personal user data profile on our own servers — account-linked data (Play Games profile and cloud save) lives on Google's infrastructure, as described in Section 2. Advertising identifiers, app interaction data, and diagnostic data are retained by our active ad mediation and diagnostic partners (currently AdMob and Unity Ads) according to their own retention policies. Local game data stored on your device can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data via your device settings.

6. Data Security

All data collected by the App and its third-party SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect the App and the limited data it processes, but no method of electronic processing or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights (EEA, UK, and Other Regions)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we (or our processors) hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — request that we limit how your data is processed.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including ad-related processing.
  • Data portability — request your data in a portable format, where technically feasible.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (e.g. personalized advertising), you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, by resetting/limiting your device's advertising identifier or adjusting the consent prompt shown in the App.

Since most ad-related and diagnostic data is collected directly by our mediation and SDK partners, requests about that data are often best directed to them via their own privacy tools (linked in Section 1), but you can also contact us at the details in Section 10 and we will assist where we can.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. If you are in Poland, this is the Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl. If you are elsewhere in the EEA/UK, you may contact your local supervisory authority instead.

8. International Data Transfers

Piegeon is based in Poland (EU). Advertising and diagnostic partners such as Google and Unity (and AppLovin, once active) may process data in other countries, including the United States, using appropriate safeguards as described in their own privacy policies.

If a future change means the App begins accessing new or additional Google user data or scopes beyond what's described in Section 2, we will update Section 2 accordingly and, where required, prompt users for consent before the change takes effect. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
